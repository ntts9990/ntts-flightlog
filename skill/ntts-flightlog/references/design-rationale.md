# Worklog Pane Design Rationale

Use these notes only when revising the worklog-pane format.

## Applied Principles

- Answer-first / executive summary: put state, focus, next step, and elapsed time at the top.
- Chunking: group information under stable headings instead of a continuous transcript.
- Signaling: use color and labels to mark turns, decisions, evidence, and blockers.
- Coherence: omit noisy raw command output and long private data excerpts.
- Closure: show turn start/end and elapsed time to reduce open-loop ambiguity.
- Plain language: keep labels short and action-oriented.

## Source Anchors

- Cambridge Handbook of Multimedia Learning, chapter on coherence, signaling, redundancy, spatial and temporal contiguity.
- FDA Plain English guidance: logical organization, common words, short paragraphs, reader-first order.
- DHS Plain Language guidance: focus on what readers need to know and organize around reader questions.
- AHRQ/IHI SBAR guidance: structured handoff via situation, background, assessment, recommendation.
- Goal-progress monitoring literature: users benefit from clear progress state and completion cues.
