# Phase E Persona Recruitment

Phase E requires evidence from three personas before GA. Recruit and record participants before writing final evidence.

## Self-Retro

Occupant: project author.

Artifact: `.omc/specs/alpha-dogfood-log.md`

Requirement: four weeks of entries, at least four distinct metric citations, and at least one `[CHANGED-BY-METRIC: metric_id]` behavior change.

## Agent-Operator

Occupant: project author or one external operator.

Artifact: `.omc/specs/v2-agent-operator-decisions.md`

Requirement: at least one Claude/Codex/Gemini comparison decision citing concrete values for at least four metrics. Run an adversarial review in a separate session and save the review notes.

## Team-Share

Occupant: at least one real external recipient.

Artifact: `.omc/specs/v2-team-share-report.md`

Requirement: send one weekly report that cites at least four metrics in plain language. Save dated acknowledgement in `docs/v2-ga-acceptance-evidence.md`.
